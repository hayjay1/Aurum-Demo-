Our Story

A kitchen born from memory and craft.

The Beginning

A chef, a memory,
and a dream.

Aurum was born in 2019 from the imagination of Chef Adaeze Nwosu — raised between Lagos and Lyon, trained in Paris, and deeply rooted in the flavours of home. After a decade cooking in Europe's finest kitchens, she returned to Nigeria with one conviction: that West African cuisine deserved to stand unapologetically at the world's finest tables.

Her grandmother's kitchen in Enugu was her first classroom. The aromas of ofe onugbu, the ritual of pounding yam at dawn, the way food brought the entire compound together — these were her foundation. Aurum is her tribute to that kitchen, rendered in a new language.

The Space

Architecture
as hospitality.

Aurum's dining room was designed by architect Tunde Eze as a conversation between Yoruba spatial traditions and contemporary minimalism. The result is a space that feels both ancient and entirely new — warm terracotta walls, hand-knotted textiles from Kano artisans, low candlelight, and long communal tables that honour the African tradition of eating together.

Every seat in the house has a clear view of the open kitchen — because at Aurum, the cooking is never hidden. It is the performance.
