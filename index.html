Our Story

A kitchen born from memory and craft.

The Beginning

A chef, a memory,
and a dream.

Aurum was born in 2019 from the imagination of Chef Adaeze Nwosu — raised between Lagos and Lyon, trained in Paris, and deeply rooted in the flavours of home. After a decade cooking in Europe's finest kitchens, she returned to Nigeria with one conviction: West African cuisine deserved to stand at the world's finest tables.

Her grandmother's kitchen in Enugu was her first classroom. The aromas of ofe onugbu, the ritual of pounding yam at dawn — these were her foundation. Aurum is her tribute, rendered in a new language.

The Space

Architecture
as hospitality.

Designed by architect Tunde Eze as a conversation between Yoruba spatial traditions and contemporary minimalism — warm terracotta walls, hand-knotted textiles from Kano artisans, low candlelight, and long communal tables that honour the African tradition of eating together.

Every seat commands a view of the open kitchen. The cooking is never hidden. It is the performance.
